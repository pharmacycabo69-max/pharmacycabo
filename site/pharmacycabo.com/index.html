Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Are you available 24/7?

Our store operates from 8AM to 6PM, while delivery coordination is available around the clock for last-minute travel essentials and guest convenience requests.

How can I pay?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, wire transfer, and cash in USD or MXN. A card terminal can be brought to the handoff.

Can you deliver to my hotel or villa?

Yes. We coordinate with you for lobby, suite, villa gate, or another property-approved handoff point.

Do you deliver to yachts?

Yes, at Cabo San Lucas Marina and Puerto Los Cabos. Please share your slip number and captain or crew contact.

Does your team speak English?

Yes. Our entire guest-support and delivery team is bilingual in English and Spanish.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no minimum. The delivery fee depends on your zone and time and is disclosed upfront.
